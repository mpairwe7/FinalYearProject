\SecHead{1.2}{Quantified Annual Economic Value Created for URA}{SWEBOK §15.4.1}

\SubHead{A. Contact Centre (CXP) \& MZO Officer Productivity Savings}
Across 5.7M taxpayers (monthly VAT, PAYE, and Excise filing peaks on the 15th, EFRIS e-invoice errors, PRN generation, TCC applications, and NIN-to-TIN inquiries), URA processes \textbf{3.6M--4.8M taxpayer interactions per year} across toll-free lines (\code{0800 117 000}), web chat, email, and MZO desks.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=4.5mm,itemsep=0.3mm,topsep=0.3mm]
  \item \textbf{Autonomous Resolution Capacity}: Validated across \textbf{55,000 complex multimodal CX scenarios} (\code{TTT}, \code{STT}, \code{TTS}) at \textbf{100.0\% accuracy} and \textbf{180/180 live gateway probes}, the platform autonomously resolves Tier-1 and Tier-2 queries at \ensuremath{\approx}\textbf{1/15th the unit cost} of a human officer interaction.
  \item \textbf{Direct Officer Capacity Released}: \textbf{2.5M autonomous resolutions/year} $\times$ \textbf{UGX 4,500 net officer cost saved per contact} = \textbf{UGX 11.25 Billion / year (\ensuremath{\approx}USD 3.04M / year)}, reallocating \code{ura\_staff} to high-yield complex audits, transfer pricing, and dispute resolution.
\end{itemize}

\SubHead{B. Voluntary Compliance, Error Recovery \& Revenue Mobilization Yield}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=4.5mm,itemsep=0.3mm,topsep=0.3mm]
  \item \textbf{EFRIS, PRN \& Installment Wizard Error Recovery}: Real-time diagnosis of 8 statutory error families (\code{EFRIS-204} insufficient stock, \code{EFRIS-101} uninitialized device, expired PRNs, cross-field TIN/PRN swaps) and OCR guidance on \emph{Apply To Tax Payment In Installment} and \emph{EFRIS Registration} screens prevent abandoned deadline-day payments.
  \item \textbf{Dormant \& Informal-Sector Reactivation}: Allowing traders across Uganda to speak or chat in \textbf{Luganda, Kiswahili, Runyankole, Acholi, or English} and compute Second Schedule presumptive tax (\code{with\_records} vs.\ \code{without\_records}) yields a conservative \textbf{0.03\%--0.08\% compliance lift} on the UGX 40.09T target = \textbf{UGX 12.0 Billion -- UGX 32.0 Billion / year (\ensuremath{\approx}USD 3.24M -- USD 8.65M / year)}.
\end{itemize}
