The PDPO describes registration and renewal, DPO appointment, DPIA and foreign
processor due diligence in its [organisation information
centre](https://www.pdpo.go.ug/information-center/organisation). Use its
[official application/renewal form](https://www.pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/01/31122021140148-Form_2_-_Application_for_Registration_Renewal_of_Registration.pdf)
and retain the authoritative evidence in the governance system.

Production startup requires both `PDPO_REGISTRATION_STATUS` (`confirmed` or
`not_required`) and `PDPO_REGISTRATION_REFERENCE`. That is an operational
attestation so an unreviewed deployment cannot silently represent itself as
compliant; it is not proof of a filing.
