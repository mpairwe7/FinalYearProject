## 1. Privacy Notice

### URA Chat Bot – Privacy Notice

**Effective Date**: February 2026
**Data Controller**: Uganda Revenue Authority (URA), Plot 1–3 Apollo Kaggwa Road, Kampala, Uganda
**Contact**: dataprotection@ura.go.ug

#### 1.1 What This Notice Covers

This Privacy Notice explains how the URA Chat Bot ("the Service") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you interact with it. The Service is an AI-powered assistant that helps you find information about Ugandan tax obligations, customs procedures, and URA services.

#### 1.2 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases (NDPA 2019 §8; GDPR Art. 6 for reference):

| Data Type | Legal Basis | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Chat messages (text) | Public interest (NDPA 2019 §8(1)(e) – public service delivery) | Provide relevant tax answers |
| Conversation session ID | Public interest | Maintain conversation context within a session |
| Browser speech-to-text output | Consent (explicit user action) | Convert voice input to text queries |
| Anonymised usage analytics | Public interest | Improve service quality |

#### 1.3 What Data We Collect

| Data Category | Collected? | Details |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| Chat message text | Yes | The text you type or dictate; used to generate a response |
| Audio recordings | **No** | Speech recognition is processed entirely in your browser; no audio is sent to our servers |
| Personal identifiers (name, TIN, NIN) | **No** – by design | We do not ask for or store personal identifiers; if you include them in a message, they are not persisted beyond the session |
| IP address | Minimal | Standard web server logs; retained for 7 days for security monitoring |
| Cookies | No | The Service does not use cookies or tracking technologies |
| Conversation history | Session only | Stored in browser memory; cleared when you close the tab |

#### 1.4 How We Use Your Data

- **Respond to your queries**: Your message is processed by our AI model to retrieve relevant tax information.
- **Improve the service**: Anonymised, aggregated query patterns may be analysed to improve FAQ coverage.
- **Security monitoring**: Server logs help detect and prevent abuse.

We do **not** use your data for:
- Marketing or advertising
- Profiling or automated decision-making with legal effects
- Sale to third parties
- Training the AI model on individual user conversations

#### 1.5 Data Retention

| Data | Retention Period |
|------|-----------------|
| Chat session data | Duration of browser session (ephemeral) |
| Server access logs | 7 days |
| Anonymised analytics | 12 months |

#### 1.6 Data Protection Officer (NDPA 2019 §35)

In accordance with NDPA 2019 §35, URA has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who is responsible for ensuring compliance with data protection obligations. The DPO can be contacted at **dpo@ura.go.ug**.

#### 1.7 Data Breach Notification (NDPA 2019 §33)

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects, URA will:
- Notify the Personal Data Protection Office within **48 hours** of becoming aware of the breach
- Notify affected data subjects without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms
- Document the breach, its effects, and the remedial actions taken in the incident register

#### 1.8 Your Rights (NDPA 2019 §20–25)

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data held by us
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") – request deletion of your data
- **Restriction** of processing
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Lodge a complaint** with the National Information Technology Authority – Uganda (NITA-U) or the Personal Data Protection Office

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **dataprotection@ura.go.ug** or use the complaint handling process described below.

#### 1.9 Data Transfers

The AI model is hosted on infrastructure that may include cloud services outside Uganda. All data transfers comply with NDPA 2019 §19 (cross-border transfer requirements). Appropriate safeguards include:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Contractual data processing agreements with cloud providers

#### 1.10 Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

#### 1.11 Changes to This Notice

We will update this notice when our data practices change. The "Effective Date" above reflects the latest version.

## 2. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

### URA Chat Bot – Acceptable Use Policy

#### 2.1 Permitted Uses

The URA Chat Bot is designed to:
- Answer general questions about Ugandan tax obligations
- Provide guidance on customs procedures and declarations
- Help you find information about URA services, systems (EFRIS, e-Tax), and processes
- Direct you to relevant URA resources and contact points

#### 2.2 Limitations

The Chat Bot:
- **Is NOT a substitute for professional tax advice**. Always consult a qualified tax advisor for complex matters.
- **Does NOT provide legally binding rulings**. Only the URA Commissioner General can issue binding rulings.
- **May contain errors**. AI-generated responses are based on publicly available URA documents and may not reflect the most recent legal changes.
- **Cannot process transactions**. You cannot file returns, make payments, or register for TIN through the chatbot.

#### 2.3 Prohibited Uses

You must NOT use the Chat Bot to:

1. **Submit personal data** – Do not enter your TIN, NIN, bank account numbers, or other sensitive personal information.
2. **Attempt to manipulate the AI** – Do not use prompt injection, jailbreaking, or adversarial inputs to alter the chatbot's behaviour.
3. **Abuse the service** – Do not send automated/scripted requests, conduct denial-of-service attacks, or attempt to extract the AI model weights.
4. **Generate harmful content** – Do not use the chatbot to create misleading, fraudulent, or offensive content.
5. **Circumvent security controls** – Do not attempt to bypass rate limits, access controls, or other protective measures.

#### 2.4 Consequences of Misuse

Violation of this AUP may result in:
- Temporary or permanent blocking of access
- Reporting to relevant law enforcement authorities
- Civil or criminal liability under Ugandan law

#### 2.5 Disclaimer

Responses are generated by an AI system and are provided "AS IS" without warranty. URA is not liable for any loss, damage, or penalty arising from reliance on chatbot responses. Always verify critical tax information through official URA channels.

## 3. Complaint Handling Workflow

### 3.1 Procedure

#### Step 1: User Submits Complaint
- **Channels**: Email (dataprotection@ura.go.ug), URA helpline (0800 117 000), or in-person at URA offices
- **Required information**: Description of concern, date/time of interaction, screenshots (if applicable)

#### Step 2: Acknowledgement
- Automated acknowledgement within **24 hours**
- Unique complaint reference number assigned

#### Step 3: Triage & Classification
- Complaint classified by type:
  - **Data Protection** (privacy, erasure, access requests)
  - **Accuracy** (incorrect tax information provided)
  - **Service Quality** (slow response, system errors)
  - **Safety** (harmful or inappropriate chatbot output)
  - **Accessibility** (language, disability access issues)

#### Step 4: Investigation
- Data Protection complaints: escalated to URA Data Protection Officer (DPO)
- Accuracy complaints: reviewed by Taxpayer Services Division + ML Team
- Safety complaints: immediate model output review; harmful patterns added to blocklist
- Target investigation period: **10 working days**

#### Step 5: Resolution & Response
- Written response to complainant with findings and actions taken
- If remediation required: timeline for fix communicated
- If complaint rejected: clear reasoning provided with appeal options

#### Step 6: Appeal
- Complainant may appeal to the URA Commissioner General
- For data protection matters: complainant may lodge a complaint with NITA-U / Personal Data Protection Office

#### Step 7: Continuous Improvement
- All complaints logged in the incident register
- Monthly review of complaint trends
- Systemic issues trigger model retraining or FAQ corpus updates
